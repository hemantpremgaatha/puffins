The Observation Behind Puffins

"Why can't a rice cake
just be a snack?"

Rice had always been a part of our founder's world. He just hadn't thought of it as a snack — until one observation about how flavourless the format felt became the starting point for every Puffins flavour.

So, somewhere we thought — what can be light, tasty, healthy and easy to carry? That's when we found rice cakes.

We noticed something interesting about rice cakes. The product was already familiar, but the way people thought about it felt limited. We saw an opportunity to change that.

What if rice cakes could be flavourful, exciting, easy to carry and genuinely enjoyable — anytime? That thought became Puffins.

"We didn't invent rice cakes. We just imagined a better way to enjoy them."
