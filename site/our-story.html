The Observation Behind Puffins

"Why can't a rice cake
just be a snack?"

Rice had always been a part of our founder's world. He just hadn't thought of it as a snack — until one observation about how flavourless the format felt became the starting point for every Puffins flavour.

Rice has always been a part of our world. We just saw it differently.

For generations, rice has been at the heart of the business behind Samrthaya Innovative Foods.

But we kept coming back to one question:

What else could rice become?

That question led us to puffed rice — light, crisp, versatile, and familiar, yet surprisingly unexplored as a modern food experience.

We began experimenting with it. Different forms. Different flavours. Different ways of enjoying it.

And Puffins was born.

Not to make another snack that simply looks better, but to create something people genuinely want to eat.

Something that can fit into a busy morning, a quick meal, or a moment when you simply want something delicious.

Puffins is our way of reimagining rice for the way we eat today.

From a grain we have known for generations to a food we are only beginning to imagine.
