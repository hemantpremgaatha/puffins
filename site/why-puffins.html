The Perception Gap

Rice cakes are ready
to be seen as a snack.

Today, rice cakes are seen as functional, plain and occasional. We believe they can be flavourful, exciting, anytime and snackable — without changing the rice cake itself.

Today's perception

Functional, plain, occasional

  • Health focused (not indulgent)
  • Often seen as an alternative or side
  • Limited flavours
  • Usually not an anytime snacking choice
Tomorrow's perception

Flavourful, exciting, anytime

  • Delicious & craveable
  • A snack people look forward to
  • Wide range of exciting flavours
  • Perfect for every moment, every mood

How it's made

From puffed rice to a pack that stays crunchy.

Puffed rice base

Starts with real puffed rice — the same light, airy grain at the heart of every Puffins format.

Puffed, not fried

Puffed to get the crunch, without the oil — that's what keeps it light.

Seasoned

Flavour is added to turn a plain format into something craveable.

Sealed to stay crisp

Packed and sealed to lock in the crunch until the pack is opened.

🔥Puffed, not fried 🌾Gluten free 🛡️Zero preservatives 🌱100% vegetarian

Health benefits

Genuinely good for you, not just labelled that way.

Puffed instead of fried, nothing artificial added to preserve it, and a format that's naturally light — health here isn't a marketing claim, it's how the snack is actually made.
